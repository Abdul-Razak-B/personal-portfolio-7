01. Flagship Research Publication

Peer-Reviewed Paper Volume 4, Issue 4 (2026) Computer Vision / CNN

DEEPLNET: A Lightweight Convolutional Neural Network for Real-Time Facial Emotion Recognition

Author: Abdul Razak Bhutto

Abstract: Real-time facial emotion recognition demands substantial computational throughput, rendering standard deep convolutional architectures inefficient for low-power edge devices and mobile systems. This research introduces DEEPLNET, an ultra-compact convolutional neural network engineered with optimized kernel depth and streamlined spatial feature extraction matrices. DEEPLNET minimizes parameter volume while sustaining high classification accuracy across baseline facial expression datasets under varying illumination and computational constraints.
